# A Bridgerton Union

Create a wedding website. The wedding is Bridgerton-themed, with a soft pastel pallette and classy, vintage typography. The landing pahe should elicit emotion and anticipation for the upcoming day with a countdown to July 24th 2027. When entering the webpage, there should be an animation of opening an envelope and sliding out the card inside, before the page zoomes in and fits the text to the screen. The webpage needs to contain the following information, easily accessible, clearly structured and seperated (for now, fill with placeholder information): Home, Our Story, Schedule, Travel & Accomodations, FAQ, Photobook (photos of the couple over the years presented visually as flipping through a photbook), and RSVP. The RSVP feature should track guest attendance and save who is coming with Supabase.
